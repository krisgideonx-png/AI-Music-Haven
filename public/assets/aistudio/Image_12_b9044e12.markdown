## Brand & Style
The design system embodies the future of sonic composition: a symbiotic ecosystem where advanced neural synthesis meets human musical artistry. Built for music producers, prompt engineers, sound designers, and audiophiles, the aesthetic projects authority, high fidelity, and hyper-modern precision.

The visual direction merges **Cyber-Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Glassmorphism**. Interfaces feature deep, low-reflectance obsidian substrates punctuated by luminous, energy-emitting spectral accents. Surfaces avoid flat monotony through ultra-fine translucent panels, hairline luminescent borders, and controlled atmospheric glows that mirror the physics of high-end hardware synthesizers and spectral audio visualizers. Interactions must feel instant, tactile, and mathematically precise, conveying deep processing power without sensory overload.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on a calculated gradient of darkness paired with precise chromatic telemetry:

- **Substrate & Neutrals:**
  - `Canvas / Root`: `#08080C` (Absolute obsidian base)
  - `Surface 01 / Panel`: `#0F0F16` (Deep charcoal structural backdrops)
  - `Surface 02 / Elevated`: `#161622` (Interactive cards, docks, and modals)
  - `Surface Glass`: `rgba(22, 22, 34, 0.72)` with backdrop blur
  - `Border Hairline`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
  - `Border Luminescent`: `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)` to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`

- **Spectral Accents:**
  - `Electric Violet` (`#8B5CF6`): Primary neural energy; utilized for primary CTAs, master gain highlights, and primary stems.
  - `Neon Teal / Cyan` (`#06B6D4` / `#22D3EE`): Secondary dynamic signal; tracks, playheads, audio wave frequencies, and generation states.
  - `Sonic Amber` (`#F59E0B`): Tertiary modulation; peak levels, warm analog modes, featured tracks, and token balances.
  - `Neural Emerald` (`#10B981`): Precision state; model training complete, audio export ready, stem isolation verified.

Always use luminescent accent tones sparingly over pure obsidian backdrops to preserve visual hierarchy and avoid visual noise.

## Typography
The typographical hierarchy balances futuristic display geometry with clinical data legibility:

- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Conveys algorithmic sophistication and structural power. Headings employ tight letter spacing to ground content sections with architectural weight.
- **Body Text (`Inter`):** Delivers transparent, neutral, and strain-free reading across dark surfaces. Used for prompt descriptions, community forum discussions, and track documentation.
- **Telemetry & Labels (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to technical metadata, audio parameters (BPM, Key, Sample Rate, Model Iteration, Latency, Timestamps). Tabular figures are enforced for real-time counters and frequency metrics.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout leverages a 12-column dynamic fluid grid for studio workstations and desktop interfaces, collapsing to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Studio Breakpoint (`= 1440px`):** Accommodates persistent left-hand navigation (64px collapsed / 240px expanded), central dynamic feed or arrangement timeline, and right-hand telemetry/prompt inspector (320px).
- **Responsive Stacking:** Below 1024px, the inspector moves to an off-canvas drawer. The persistent bottom audio dock remains anchored across all viewports, preserving a mandatory `80px` (desktop) or `72px` (mobile) bottom padding cushion across the main scroll container.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Root spacing operates on a baseline 4px/8px modular rhythm, ensuring waveforms, sliders, parameter meters, and track row heights maintain optical balance.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed through atmospheric optical layering rather than physical skeuomorphic shadows:

1. **Layer 0 (Substrate):** `#08080C` matte backdrop with optional micro-radial background gradients centered behind prominent waveform visualizers.
2. **Layer 1 (Panels & Feed):** `#0F0F16` solid or `rgba(15, 15, 22, 0.85)` with `16px` backdrop blur and hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
3. **Layer 2 (Cards & Track Cells):** `#161622` glass panels with dual inner glow: `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
4. **Layer 3 (Floating Docks & Modals):** `rgba(22, 22, 34, 0.90)` backed by `24px` backdrop blur, a micro-border of `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)`, and a deep, low-luminance drop shadow: `0 16px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 20px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.08)`.
5. **Interactive Glow (Hover/Active):** Interactive elements evoke soft luminescent halation (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)` or `0 0 16px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)`), reinforcing raw electric energy.

## Shapes
A baseline roundedness of **Level 2** (`8px` base, `16px` for containers) provides a balanced, contemporary silhouette that softens the aggressive technical tone without sacrificing precision.

- **Badges, Pills, and Tags:** Pill radius (`9999px`) is strictly reserved for metadata tags, playback speed buttons, and prompt status pills to signal interactive affordances.
- **Cards, Stems, and Waveform Enclosures:** `rounded-lg` (`16px`) creates refined, modern capsules for complex audio data.
- **Inner Controls (Knobs, Faders, Segmented Toggles):** `rounded` (`8px`) or `rounded-sm` (`4px`) provides strict mechanical precision.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Triggers
- **Primary CTA (Generate / Mint):** Solid electric violet (`#8B5CF6`) background with white text, transitioning to cyan hover with a soft luminescent bloom (`0 0 18px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.5)`). Height: 44px (desktop), 40px (compact).
- **Secondary (Inspect / Fork Stem):** Dark surface `#161622` with a 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover triggers border transition to `#06B6D4`.
- **Ghost / Transport Controls:** Borderless icon buttons with translucent states; active icons display glowing violet or cyan illumination.

### 2. Audio Metadata Chips
- Encapsulated pills in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` with a `1px` border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Use `JetBrains Mono` at `label-mono-sm` (11px).
- Metadata-specific color indicators:
  - **BPM / Key:** Cyan text dot (`#06B6D4`).
  - **AI Model (e.g., v4.2-Neural):** Electric violet dot (`#8B5CF6`).
  - **Mood / Genre:** Translucent text with white pill outline.
  - **Vocal Type:** Amber dot (`#F59E0B`).
  - **License (CC-BY / Commercial):** Subtle emerald badge (`#10B981`).

### 3. Waveform Audio Cards
- Surface: Glassmorphic `#161622` with `16px` corner radius and `1px` boundary border.
- Waveform Visualizer: Multi-bar canvas or SVG with dual coloration—unplayed region in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`, played region in dynamic `#06B6D4` fading back into `#8B5CF6`.
- Integrated micro-actions: Inline play/pause trigger, stem expansion button, clone prompt icon, and real-time scrubber cursor.

### 4. Inputs & Prompt Engine Fields
- Dark input fields (`#0F0F16`) with inner shadow, inset by `1px` border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#8B5CF6` accompanied by an ambient ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.2)`.
- Parameter sliders (Creativity, CFG Scale, Seed): High-contrast slim track (`4px`) in `#161622` with active fill in `#06B6D4` and glowing round handle (`14px`).

### 5. Persistent Audio Master Player
- Fixed bottom docked bar across viewports with `rgba(15, 15, 22, 0.92)` blur backing.
- Top border features a 1px hairline gradient (`transparent` -> `#8B5CF6` -> `#06B6D4` - `transparent`).
- Left: Mini-artwork with title, creator tag, and vocal model badge.
- Center: Master timeline scrubber with real-time waveform bars, loop range indicators, and mono timestamp readouts.
- Right: Master volume fader, stem mixer toggle, spectral visualizer modal trigger, and high-res export options
